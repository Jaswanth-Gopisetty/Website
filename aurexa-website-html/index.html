About Aurexa Technologies

What makes us the trusted partner for compliance-driven digital transformation

Our team brings deep expertise in GxP principles, 21 CFR Part 11, ISO 13485, EU MDR and other critical regulatory frameworks. We don't just build software — we architect digital ecosystems aligned with audit expectations, operational realities and inspection requirements. Every solution is grounded in real-world scenarios: deviations that require immediate escalation, training records that must remain immutable, audit trails that inspectors expect to see in minutes, not hours.

This domain fluency means faster implementations, smarter configurations and platforms that feel intuitive to quality managers, compliance officers, auditors and regulatory affairs professionals. When you work with Aurexa, you work with a team that speaks your language — and understands the stakes.

Every transaction in our systems generates an immutable audit trail — timestamped, attributed and tamper-proof. Role-based access controls ensure only authorised users can view, edit or approve sensitive records. Electronic signatures meet 21 CFR Part 11 requirements. Approval workflows enforce multi-level review chains before changes take effect. Version control safeguards documents against unintended modifications.

Our infrastructure meets SOC 2, ISO 27001 and GDPR standards. Hosting environments are designed for high availability, disaster recovery and business continuity — because downtime in regulated industries is not an option. With Aurexa, you don't retrofit compliance into a generic tool. You deploy a platform engineered from the ground up to meet — and exceed — the expectations of auditors, regulators and internal quality teams.

Our platforms leverage cloud-native architecture, AI-powered automation and real-time analytics to surface insights, reduce manual overhead and accelerate decision-making — without sacrificing the rigour regulators demand. Intelligent workflows route deviations, training tasks and approval requests to the right people at the right time. Dashboards provide live visibility into CAPA status, audit readiness and compliance KPIs. Integration APIs connect Aurexa seamlessly with existing ERP, LIMS, MES and BI systems — creating a unified digital thread across your operations.

As your organisation evolves, our platforms scale with you — supporting new sites, additional workflows, custom fields and compliance requirements unique to your industry. Whether you're a mid-sized manufacturer preparing for FDA inspection or a global biopharma managing multi-site quality operations, Aurexa delivers the agility, security and control you need to thrive in a regulated world.
