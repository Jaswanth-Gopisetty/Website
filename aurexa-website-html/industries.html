Industries we serve

At Aurexa Technologies we specialize in serving highly regulated industries where quality, compliance, and operational control are not optional — they are fundamental. Our platforms are designed to meet the stringent regulatory demands of these industries, helping organizations improve efficiency, maintain continuous compliance, and scale with confidence.

Pharmaceuticals

Pharmaceuticals

Aurexa supports pharmaceutical companies with GxP-aligned platforms for Quality, Document Control, Training, CAPA, Change Control, Deviations, and Batch Management. Ensure compliance with FDA 21 CFR Part 11, ICH, and global regulatory standards while improving operational efficiency and reducing inspection risk.

Medical Devices

Medical Devices

Aurexa enables Medical Device manufacturers to manage Design Controls, Risk Management, Supplier Management, and Post-Market Surveillance in a unified system. Ensure compliance with ISO 13485, FDA QSR, and MDR while accelerating product development and maintaining traceability across the device lifecycle.

Biotechnology

Biotechnology

Aurexa helps Biotech companies streamline R&D, lab operations, and regulatory documentation with platforms built for complex workflows, cross-functional collaboration, and compliance traceability. From discovery through commercialization, our solutions adapt to the pace and rigor of biotech innovation.

Chemicals

Chemicals

Aurexa supports Chemical manufacturers with solutions for SDS Management, Safety Compliance, Environmental Monitoring, and Quality Control. Ensure alignment with REACH, OSHA, and ISO 9001 while managing complex formulations, supplier chains, and production workflows with transparency and control.

Food & Beverage

Food & Beverage

Aurexa enables Food & Beverage companies to manage HACCP Plans, Supplier Audits, Allergen Control, and Traceability with digital precision. Ensure compliance with FSMA, GFSI, and regional food safety regulations while improving consistency, reducing recalls, and maintaining consumer trust.

Electronics & Semiconductors

Electronics & Semiconductors

Aurexa supports Electronics & Semiconductor manufacturers with solutions for Statistical Process Control (SPC), Cleanroom Monitoring, Failure Analysis, and Quality Audits. Maintain compliance with IATF 16949, ISO 9001, and industry standards while optimizing yield and product reliability.

Cosmetics

Cosmetics

Aurexa helps Cosmetics companies manage Formulation Records, Stability Testing, Safety Assessments, and Global Regulatory Documentation. Ensure compliance with FDA OTC regulations, EU Cosmetics Regulation, and international requirements while accelerating time-to-market and maintaining brand reputation.

Aerospace

Aerospace

Aurexa supports Aerospace organizations with platforms for Configuration Management, Supplier Qualification, Non-Conformance Tracking, and Airworthiness Documentation. Ensure compliance with AS9100, FAA, and EASA while managing complex supply chains, change approvals, and safety-critical processes.

Cannabis

Cannabis

Aurexa enables Cannabis companies to navigate evolving state and federal regulations with platforms for Seed-to-Sale Tracking, Lab Testing, Packaging & Labeling, and Compliance Reporting. Ensure alignment with state cannabis regulations, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), and emerging federal standards.

Healthcare

Healthcare

Aurexa supports Healthcare providers with solutions for Clinical Quality, Credentialing, Adverse Event Tracking, and Regulatory Documentation. Ensure compliance with HIPAA, Joint Commission, CMS, and accreditation standards while improving patient safety, operational efficiency, and audit readiness.
