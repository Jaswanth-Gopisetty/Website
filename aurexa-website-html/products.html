Our Products

Aurexa Technologies delivers industry-focused digital products tailored for regulated and compliance-driven organizations. Our flagship platform, QCMetric, represents the future of quality management in regulated industries.

Core Capabilities

  • AI-powered Quality Management: Intelligent automation, risk prediction, and compliance monitoring
  • CAPA Management: Root cause analysis, corrective & preventive actions with full traceability
  • Change Control & Deviations: Structured workflows with impact assessment and approval chains
  • Document Management System: Version control, e-signatures, automated distribution & retention
  • Training Management: Curriculum planning, competency tracking, automated reminders & reporting
  • Dynamic Forms & Workflows: Configurable forms, conditional logic, multi-stage approvals
  • Audit Trail & Reporting: Immutable logs, real-time KPIs, compliance dashboards, regulatory reports
  • Compliance Alignment: GxP · FDA 21 CFR Part 11 · ISO 9001 · ISO 13485 · GDPR · HIPAA ready

Built for Regulated Industries

QCMetric is purpose-built for organizations operating under stringent regulatory oversight. Our platform helps pharmaceutical manufacturers, medical device companies, biotech firms, and other regulated entities maintain continuous compliance while improving operational efficiency and quality outcomes.

Technology Stack

QCMetric is built on a modern, cloud-native architecture designed for security, scalability, and reliability. Our platform leverages advanced AI capabilities, real-time analytics, and enterprise-grade security controls to deliver a robust, inspection-ready eQMS solution.
