About Aurexa Technologies

Founded with a vision to transform how regulated organisations manage quality, compliance and operational risk, Aurexa Technologies brings deep domain expertise, technical excellence and a compliance-first mindset to every solution we deliver.

Aurexa's team brings extensive experience across life sciences, pharmaceuticals, biotech, medical devices, healthcare, manufacturing and other compliance-intensive sectors. We understand the language, regulations and operational challenges these industries face daily.

Every system we build is designed with validation, audit trails, data integrity and regulatory readiness at its core. From 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11 to ISO 13485 and ICH-GCP, we ensure your digital infrastructure meets stringent global standards from day one.

Whether you're a mid-sized manufacturer or a global pharmaceutical enterprise, Aurexa's platforms are built to grow with you. We leverage cloud-native architecture, microservices, and modular design to ensure scalability, resilience and flexibility as your organization evolves.
